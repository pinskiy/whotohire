Privacy Policy

Effective from 27 October 2026

In short: this site requires no registration, does not collect names, phone numbers or email addresses, sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Your answers stay in your browser, are never sent anywhere, and the author cannot see them.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Leonid Losich, Poland. For any questions about your data or this site, contact: .

2. What data is processed and why

2.1. Hosting data

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, when you open a page your browser sends the server your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page address and information about your browser. This data is processed by the hosting provider, GitHub, Inc. (USA), to deliver the page and protect the service against attacks. The author of the site has no access to these logs and does not use them.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in the operation and security of the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is transferred to the United States. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer is covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision. More details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

2.2. Data stored in your browser

So that your progress is not lost when you reload the page, your answers, the current question and the chosen tone are saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This data stays on your device only and is not shared with the author or any third party.

This storage is strictly necessary for a feature you are actively using, so no consent is required (Art. 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC). You can delete this data with the "Пройти заново" (Start over) button or by clearing site data in your browser settings.

2.3. Fonts, links and cards

Fonts are loaded from the same server as the site, without contacting any third-party services. The "Поделиться результатом" (Share result) link contains only the name of the role, for example "#pr", and none of your answers. The social media card and the job description draft are created in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

2.4. Correspondence

If you write to the author, your email address and the content of your message are processed in order to reply. Legal basis: legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Messages are kept for as long as the conversation lasts and deleted once it has ended and they are no longer needed.

3. External websites

The page contains a plain link to the author's LinkedIn profile. The site does not embed any LinkedIn widgets and does not send any data to LinkedIn. If you follow the link, LinkedIn's privacy policy applies.

4. Automated decision-making

The quiz result is calculated in your browser using predefined rules. It is a recommendation, not a decision that produces legal effects for you. No profiling takes place and your answers are not stored anywhere.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify or erase your data, to restrict its processing, to object to processing and to receive your data in a portable format. To exercise these rights, write to . Apart from correspondence, the author does not hold any visitor data. Hosting logs are held only by GitHub.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl). You may also contact the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live.

6. Changes to this policy

If the way the site processes data changes, this policy will be updated before the changes take effect. The date of the current version is shown at the top of this page.
